Modirum Platforms · Critical communications

Designing situational awareness for complex operations

I design experiences across a critical communications portfolio, with a primary focus on NSC3—a platform supporting control-room operators and mobile field personnel.

RoleUX design · Product collaboration

FocusSituational awareness · Operator workflows

Context

NSC3 brings communication and operational information together for people coordinating a response in demanding environments. Its users work across control rooms and the field, sometimes with limited connectivity.

My contribution

I work with product leadership, developers, and end users to turn evolving needs into coherent product capabilities. I translate ambiguous requirements into specifications and clickable prototypes that make ideas easier to inspect and discuss.

Design and development now progress together. Early prototypes help the team review the experience, start feasible work sooner, and adapt as we learn from working increments.

Building the way we build

I’m helping develop a shared design system and an AI-assisted design and development workflow. Together, these support faster prototyping and give developers clearer, reusable foundations for implementation.
