About

I’m Pietari Pakarinen, UX designer with a foundation in software engineering, specializing in creating usable, technically robust interfaces for complex systems. I’ve worked in roles ranging from coding to teaching to project management.

My tendency to prioritise the user experience of the software I built has led me to broaden my expertise from development to design. Now, whether it’s defining interaction models, building prototypes, or aligning design with engineering constraints, I solve problems where usability meets technical depth.

My experience spans defence technology, enterprise software, and research. I thrive in interdisciplinary teams where design and engineering intersect, defining requirements from ambiguous to intricate and them into clear, effective experiences.

When I’m not doing all that, you’ll usually instead find me engaging in some nerdy hobby, like bouldering, Dungeons & Dragons or Magic the Gathering.
